Compatibility
 
Please note that the following documentations are relevant only for OpenERP 7. From Bahmni 0.92 onwards, we use Odoo version 10.

For Odoo configuration please refer [Odoo modules](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/BAH/pages/899973149/Odoo+modules)

Bahmni integrates OpenMRS with OpenERP which is a an open source ERP product. It is useful in Billing, Pharmacy and Stock management. Any patient registered in OpenMRS is synced by Bahmni as a customer in OpenERP. **
 
Since Odoo/OpenERP is an open source product with thousands of existing customers and a community – its own online documentation is also available online here: [https://doc.odoo.com/book/#books-link](https://doc.odoo.com/book/#books-link). You can also refer to this documentation to understand OpenERP better, or in cases where you need more clarity than the documentation provided by Bahmni on OpenERP.
