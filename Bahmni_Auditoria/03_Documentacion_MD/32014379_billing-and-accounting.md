## Purpose and Benefits

Using Bahmni, a doctor can prescribe drugs/medicines or place orders for patient to undergo some tests for a patient from the clinical module or consultation feature. The patient then proceeds to the Pharmacy or to a Billing Counter to make the payment. This module describes the usage of the Billing and Accounting feature.

 
For additional information, please refer to [Oddo / OpenERP documentation](https://www.erponline.vn/en/documentation/user-manuals/88-accounting-financial/216-customer-invoicing-payments) or the [OpenERP e-book](https://doc.odoo.com/book/#books-link)
