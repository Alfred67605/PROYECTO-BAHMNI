# Preparation for Bahmni inception

> **Fuente Oficial:** [Atlassian Confluence Wiki - Bahmni](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/BAH/pages/19234865)  
> **Versión del documento:** v6 (2021-12-23T06:21:10.490Z) | **Autor/Editor:** Gurpreet Luthra

---

Inception is time boxed product envisioning workshop to capture software requirements, build backlog, prioritize, and, come up with the minimum viable product, to help create a release & rollout plan.

### Stationary to carry

- Flip-chart paper pad or sticky-backed giant post-it note pad - 1 (10 pages at least)
- Calendar - 1
- Black Sharpies/Sketch-pen - 5 
- Coloured Sharpies/Sketch-pen - 5
- Black White board markers - 2
- Coloured White board markers - 3
- White board eraser -  1
- Paper pads - 5
- Pens - 10
- Blue tack glue -1
- Post-it notes, various sizes, colours -  5
- White coloured index cards - 4" x 6” dimension - 2 packs(50 each)
- Coloured index cards - 4" x 6" dimension - 2 packs(50 each)
