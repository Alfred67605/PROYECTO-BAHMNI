## Purpose and Benefits

The In-Patient Management Module (IPD) is used to manage the admission, discharge and transfer of patients. It includes ward management and bed allocation.**
 
With 0.91 release, a [new Bed Management feature](/wiki/spaces/BAH/pages/699400201/Bed+Management+BM) is available. While the older feature is also available, we strongly advice any existing implementation to transition to the new feature soon. Going forward, as soon as we reach feature parity, this module will be deprecated. The deprecation is intended for Release 0.93 or so.
