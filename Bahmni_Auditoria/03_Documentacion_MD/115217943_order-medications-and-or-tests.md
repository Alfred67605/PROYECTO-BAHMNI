## Steps

1. From the clinical dashboard, click on the Consultation tab.

![](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/download/attachments/115217943/Screenshot%202021-12-06%20at%2010.54.48%20AM.png?version=1&modificationDate=1638768475797&cacheVersion=1&api=v2)

**2. Order Medications**

Within the consultation page, choose Medication tab to order medicines as in screenshot below and click on SAVE.
 
To **order sets of Medications** please [refer to this page](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/display/BAH/Using+Order+Sets)

**

![](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/download/attachments/115217943/Screenshot%202021-12-06%20at%2010.56.21%20AM.png?version=2&modificationDate=1638768605115&cacheVersion=1&api=v2)

![](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/download/attachments/115217943/Screenshot%202021-12-06%20at%2010.56.50%20AM.png?version=1&modificationDate=1638768682961&cacheVersion=1&api=v2)

3. **Order Tests**

Within the consultation page, choose Orders tab to order tests as in screenshot below and click on SAVE.

![](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/download/attachments/115217943/Screenshot%202021-12-06%20at%2010.56.02%20AM.png?version=1&modificationDate=1638768651765&cacheVersion=1&api=v2)
