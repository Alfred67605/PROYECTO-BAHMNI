If you wish to pull information from Odoo on payments done and show them in Clinical screens, you can look at his discussion: [https://talk.openmrs.org/t/handling-pay-before-services-in-openmrs-and-bahmni/22421](https://talk.openmrs.org/t/handling-pay-before-services-in-openmrs-and-bahmni/22421). Please post your questions about this module in Bahmni/OpenMRS Talk.
