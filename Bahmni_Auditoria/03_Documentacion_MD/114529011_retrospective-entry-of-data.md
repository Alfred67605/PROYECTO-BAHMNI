## Purpose and Benefits

Bahmni allows users to enter data in "Retrospective Entry mode”. This is extremely useful for low-resource settings to support use-cases where back-dated data has to be entered due to system/power/network failure or in cases where doctors have captured drug orders on paper.
 
**Bulk** retrospective data entry is currently NOT supported.
 
Retrospective data entry is currently ONLY supported in the Clinical App.
