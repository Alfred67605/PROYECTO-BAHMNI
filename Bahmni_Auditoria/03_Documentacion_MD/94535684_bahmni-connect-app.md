## Purpose and Benefits

Bahmni Connect allows a user to access Bahmni in areas with limited internet connectivity. This is especially useful for [Community Health Workers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_health_worker) (CHWs) to use Bahmni to register patients, view medical records, and enter brief treatment data in remote, internet-lacking locations. The CHW can later come into an area with connectivity to a Bahmni server, and *synchronize* the app on their Android device/Chromebook with the Bahmni server.
