Compatibility
 
Please note that the following documentations are relevant only for OpenERP 7. From Bahmni 0.92 onwards, we use Odoo v10.

OpenERP 7 is still workable with rest of Bahmni 0.92, but requires some work and configuration. Please refer to [FAQ](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/BAH/pages/900530355/ERP+FAQs+Troubleshooting) section

**

Bahmni integrates OpenMRS with OpenERP which is a an open source ERP product. It is useful in Billing, Pharmacy and Stock management. Any patient registered in OpenMRS is synced by Bahmni as a customer in OpenERP.
