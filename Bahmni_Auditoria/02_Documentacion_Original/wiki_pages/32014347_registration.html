Registration

About the Feature

Patient Registration provides the ability to start a patient file at a hospital. Most hospitals have a front desk where patients have to register before starting medical interactions such as consultation, lab tests, admission, procedures etc. The Registration Module in Bahmni will be used at the front desk to capture patient information that can be collected by non-clinical staff. 

Used By

The person at the Registration desk of the facility would predominantly use this module.

How is it Used?

 The Registration Module allows for searching existing patients, adding new patients to the system, capturing name, photograph, address and specific attributes that the hospital may be interested in. It can also be configured to capture clinical information such as height, weight etc depending on a hospital workflow. 

Bahmni configuration for each implementation can vary drastically. As the screens and workflows are not constant across all installations of Bahmni, we have refrained from prescribing them.
