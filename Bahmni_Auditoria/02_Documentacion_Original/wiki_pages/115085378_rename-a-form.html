Rename a Form

Purpose and Benefits

 Sometimes, implementer need to change the name of one form. It's convenient for implementer to rename it by form builder.

Steps

1. Chose the form

Chose the form which need to rename from the form builder dashboard. （For example, chose the first form) 


2. Goto the Form Detail

Click on the eye icon (or pen icon) to enter the form detail page. If the status of the form is published, then click on Edit button to open the edit mode for the rename function to be available.

 Edit model

3. Rename the form

Double click on the form name, and input the new name.

Remember to save it after rename, otherwise the system won't change the form name.
