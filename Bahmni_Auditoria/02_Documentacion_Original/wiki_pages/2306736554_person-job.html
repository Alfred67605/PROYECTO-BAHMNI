Description

Preferred from person_name table in openmrs

gender

gender of person from person table in openmrs

birthyear

year of person birth

birthtime

Time of person birth

birthdate_estimated

provided date of birth is estimated or accurate, is indicated with birth_estimated ( true/false )

age

age of person from person table

dead

death status from person table ( true of false )

death_date

death date in case

deathdate_estimated

State/Province of the patient

county_district

 District of the patient’s residence

Latitude of the area of residence

longitude

Longitude of the area of residence

Date when the patient started living in that area

end_date

e) person_attributes : This table contains  all the patient attributes that where used in the registration page. Columns will be generated dynamically based on the attributes that were used as a part of that specific implementation.

Eg : givenName, familyName, isCaretakerRequired, caretakerGender
