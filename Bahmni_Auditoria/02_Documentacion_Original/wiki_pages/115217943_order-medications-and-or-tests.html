Order Medications and/or Tests

Steps

1. From the clinical dashboard, click on the Consultation tab.

2. Order Medications

Within the consultation page, choose Medication tab to order medicines as in screenshot below and click on SAVE.

To order sets of Medications please refer to this page



3. Order Tests

Within the consultation page, choose Orders tab to order tests as in screenshot below and click on SAVE.
