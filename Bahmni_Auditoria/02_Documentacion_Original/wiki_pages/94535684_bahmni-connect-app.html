Bahmni Connect App

Purpose and Benefits

Bahmni Connect allows a user to access Bahmni in areas with limited internet connectivity. This is especially useful for Community Health Workers (CHWs) to use Bahmni to register patients, view medical records, and enter brief treatment data in remote, internet-lacking locations. The CHW can later come into an area with connectivity to a Bahmni server, and synchronize the app on their Android device/Chromebook with the Bahmni server.


WARNING: Bahmni Connect App is no longer being maintained. Instead please see the Bahmni Outreach App, for Offline-ready, CHW Mobile App here: Bahmni Outreach App for Community Healthcare (Offline ready)
